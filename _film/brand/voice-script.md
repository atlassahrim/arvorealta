# Brand explainer, voice script v1

Every line is the live English copy on /brand/. Nothing is new. About 110
words in 46 seconds, roughly 2.4 words a second: an unhurried read.

| time | scene | voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0.8–5 | Open | Competence everyone can see. |
| 6.4–11.5 | Hallmark | A hallmark told a buyer the metal was good before anyone tested it. *(beat)* A well-drawn mark does the same. |
| 12.8–19 | Alignment | Can you see the difference? *(two beats, over the lockups)* Competence shows in the drawing. |
| 20.8–26.5 | System | Tools are the easy part. We keep it actionable by keeping it simple: clean vectors, not thirty-page rulebooks. |
| 28–34 | Record | Each one of a kind. *(the rest is pictures)* |
| 35.8–40 | Result | Something to stand behind. Staff who are proud of it wear it, hand out the card and defend the standard. |
| 41.4–46 | Close | Raise the standard. *(beat)* arvorealta.com. |

Direction: low, close and dry. Read it like a statement, not an advert, and
let the pauses carry the alignment and record scenes.
