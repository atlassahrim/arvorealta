# EU tender documents — what gets demanded, ranked

**Last verified: 2026-09-14.** Re-verify annually. `reference/refresh.md` holds the
procedure and the changelog. **If today is more than fourteen months past that
date, say so before relying on anything dated in this skill.**

Geography, in the priority this file is built for: **Sweden, Norway, Denmark**,
then **Estonia**, then Finland, Iceland, Latvia, Lithuania.

**Norway and Iceland are EEA, not EU.** They carry the same directive content
through the EEA agreement and their own national regulations, but they are not
bound by EU regulations directly and the forthcoming Public Procurement Act
reaches them only through EEA incorporation, on its own timetable. Never write
"EU rules" across all five countries without this caveat.

## The ranking principle

Ordered by **what happens when it is missing**, not by how much work it is:

- **Tier 0 — Gate.** Missing or defective and the bid is rejected. No discretion,
  no scoring, frequently no opportunity to correct.
- **Tier 1 — Qualification.** Decides whether you are allowed to be evaluated at
  all. In a restricted procedure this is the whole contest.
- **Tier 2 — Award.** Scored. This is where a contract is won or lost on points.
- **Tier 3 — Conditional.** Sector, threshold or buyer-specific. Absent from most
  tenders and decisive in the ones that carry it.

A document's tier can move between tenders. The same EPD is Tier 3 in one
procurement and Tier 0 in another that makes it a mandatory minimum requirement.
**Read the tender, not this list.** Where a tier is contested, the file says so.

## Tier 0 — Gate

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ESPD** (European Single Procurement Document) | Self-declaration on exclusion and selection. Mandatory above EU thresholds. Estonia calls it *hankepass*. Structured electronic form in the buyer's system, not a document you design. |
| 2 | **Tender form / price form in the buyer's own template** | Almost always a locked spreadsheet or portal form. Substituting your own format is a common rejection cause. |
| 3 | **Signature, authority and registration** | Power of attorney, company registration extract, proof the signatory can bind the company. |
| 4 | **Exclusion-grounds evidence** | Tax, social security, criminal record, insolvency. National instruments differ sharply — see `reference/country-layers.md`. Usually demanded from the winner only, before award. |
| 5 | **Mandatory technical compliance statement** | Point-by-point confirmation against the specification. Where the product is CE-marked, the Declaration of Performance or DoC belongs here. |
| 6 | **Mandatory minimum requirements, product-specific** | Anything the tender marks *skall/må/skal/pitää*. A single unmet minimum excludes regardless of score. |

## Tier 1 — Qualification

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **References — similar deliveries** | The most contested document in the whole set. Every complaint archive in `_research/voice.md` turns on it. Typically three, within three to five years, with named contact persons, scope, value and date. |
| 8 | **Financial standing** | Annual accounts, turnover thresholds, credit rating, sometimes a parent guarantee. |
| 9 | **Liability and product insurance** | Certificate with sums and validity. |
| 10 | **Capacity and organisation** | Staffing plan, key roles, delivery capacity. A general statement of intent is a known failure — see KOFA 2025/1900 in `_research/voice.md`. |
| 11 | **Reliance on other entities** | ESPD Part II C plus a binding commitment declaration from the entity relied on. Its validity at award is litigated. |
| 12 | **Quality management** | ISO 9001 or a documented equivalent system. "Or equivalent" is where disputes start. |
| 13 | **Environmental management** | ISO 14001 or EMAS, or equivalent. |
| 14 | **Occupational health and safety** | ISO 45001. In Norway historically HMS-egenerklæring — verify current status, see refresh notes. |

## Tier 2 — Award

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | **Quality response / method statement** | The scored narrative. Usually page-capped. This is the document Arvorealta's standard exists for. |
| 16 | **Environmental documentation** | EPD to EN 15804, carbon footprint, recycled content, GWP. Norway weights climate and environment at minimum 30 per cent since 1 January 2024. |
| 17 | **Delivery and implementation plan** | Dates, milestones, handover. **Scored more finely than suppliers expect** — a Danish contract turned on five days' difference in when QA documentation would be delivered (P. Olesen, `_research/voice.md`). |
| 18 | **CVs and key personnel** | Named individuals, often with a commitment to actually deploy them. |
| 19 | **Service, maintenance, spare parts, lifecycle cost** | For equipment, frequently a heavier scoring block than the product itself. |
| 20 | **Social and labour conditions** | Collective agreements, wage conditions, apprentices, supply-chain due diligence. Norway's rules on lønns- og arbeidsvilkår are the strictest in the region. |
| 21 | **Innovation / functional response** | Where the buyer states a need rather than a solution. |

## Tier 3 — Conditional

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | **Security and defence** | Facility and personnel clearance, national security authority approval, NATO codification, export control. Gate-level where it applies. |
| 23 | **Information security and data protection** | ISO 27001, DPA, hosting location, NIS2 where in scope. |
| 24 | **Accessibility** | EN 301 549 for ICT. |
| 25 | **Product passports** | Battery passport from **18 February 2027** for EV, industrial >2 kWh and light-means-of-transport batteries. ESPR passports for other categories follow their delegated acts — see the contradiction note below. |
| 26 | **Prequalification system membership** | Achilles (Sellihca, TransQ, StartBANK, JQS) and similar. Not a tender document but a precondition for being invited in utilities and energy. |
| 27 | **Contract-phase documents** | Performance bond, bank guarantee, QA plan, as-built and handover documentation. Not scored, but committed to in the bid. |

## Dated instruments a manufacturer is actually bound by

Verified 2026-09-14. **Never round or approximate these.**

| Date | Instrument | What it demands |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January 2024 | Norway, anskaffelsesforskriften § 7-9 | Climate and environment weighted **minimum 30 per cent**, follow-or-explain, for procurements announced on Doffin from that date. |
| 8 January 2026 | Construction Products Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 | Most operational requirements apply. **GWP must be declared in the Declaration of Performance** for priority categories — moving the carbon figure out of a voluntary EPD and into mandatory CE marking. |
| 18 February 2027 | Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 | Battery passport mandatory for EV, industrial >2 kWh and LMT batteries. The first legally fixed product-passport date. |
| 9 September 2026 | Proposed EU Public Procurement Act | **A proposal, not law.** A single Regulation intended to replace Directives 2014/24, 2014/25 and 2014/23. Best price-quality ratio as standard award method, quality at minimum 30 per cent (50 per cent labour-intensive), comply-or-explain. Must still pass Parliament and Council. |

**Contradiction to record, not resolve.** `_research/trigger.md` dates ESPR full
application at 19 July 2026 with an EU Central DPP Registry live. Sources
retrieved 2026-09-14 state that as of August 2026 **no ESPR delegated act had yet
been adopted**, and that each act carries a transition period of at least
eighteen months. Both cannot be describing the same thing. The framework
regulation applying and a product category actually being obliged are different
events, and that is the likely explanation — but it is not confirmed. **Do not
put an ESPR date in front of a client until this is resolved.** Battery is the
only product-passport date that is safe to state.

## How to use this

**Scoping an annex or a tier.** Tier 0 and Tier 1 are largely form-filling
against someone else's template — low design value, high exclusion risk. Tier 2
is where a document standard changes an outcome. The €600 single document should
almost always be a Tier 2 artefact; €2,400 covers the Tier 2 set plus the Tier 1
documents that recur.

**Judging a client's pack.** Walk the tiers in order. A missing Tier 0 item is an
emergency; a weak Tier 2 item is the actual job.

**Answering "what does country X want".** Read `reference/country-layers.md`. The
EU baseline is the same everywhere; the exclusion evidence and the labour-
conditions layer are not.

**Files:** `reference/document-ladder.md` — the same ladder with what each
document typically contains and its common failure modes.
`reference/country-layers.md` — per-country instruments and portals.
`reference/refresh.md` — the annual procedure, source list and changelog.
