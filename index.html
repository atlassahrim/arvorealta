06 questions

Before you ask.

Why only two weeks?

Because it is a system, not a bespoke artwork. The scope is fixed, the method is already built, and I have delivered exactly this for an industrial manufacturer before. Longer projects are usually longer because the scope was never agreed.

Our documents are in Finnish, German, Swedish. Is that a problem?

No. I design the system; you own the language. Your copy is placed verbatim, never translated or rewritten by me, and every fact is confirmed by you before anything is final. Typography and structure work the same in any language.

What if we do not like it?

Start with one document at €600 and judge the real thing before committing to the standard — and if you go on, that €600 comes off the price. On the full engagement you verify every fact before anything is final, one round of revisions is included, and the 50% split means you are never exposed for the whole amount on work you have not seen.

Who owns the files?

You do. Source files, templates and the workflow are handed over on delivery and are yours to keep, edit and reuse without me.

Do we need a designer afterwards?

No — that is the point. The templates are built for non-designers, and the last system I delivered is operated daily by engineers and sales people.

You are in Brazil. How does that work in practice?

UTC−3, which overlaps with European afternoons and the whole UK working day. The last engagement was delivered entirely async to a Finnish client in two weeks, without a single scheduled meeting.
