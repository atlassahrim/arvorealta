Findings / Sheet 2

DR-001

Four things an evaluator meets first.

Finding 01 / Krav 11 and 14, both A

One size, where six are required.

«Beholdere på 2 hjul skal være 80l, 140l, 240l og 370l eller 360l.»

The sheet covers the 240 litre two-wheel container alone. Thirteen absences follow from that.

Finding 02 / Krav 57, A · climate 30 of 100

A word, where a number is asked.

«Forpliktende minimumsandel PCR (vekt-%) oppgis separat…»

Recyclable is not a percentage. The product page claims up to 100; the sheet does not.

Finding 03 / Krav 18, A

The table contradicts the minimum.

«Hjulene skal være… med minimum diameter 250 mm (tohjuls).»

The table reads 200/250*, the larger wheel conditional on a footnote.

Finding 04 / Krav 3 and 4, both A

A mark is not a certificate.

«Beholderne skal tilfredsstille NS EN 840 del 1-6… vedlegge dokumentasjon.»

Five marks. No certificate number, no issuing body, no date, no part of the standard.

The document an evaluator reads carries the manufacturer's mark, and it was written for a catalogue, in 2020.
