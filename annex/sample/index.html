Specifications

Planter and planter-bench units in S355J2 structural steel to EN 10025-2. Fabrication is CE marked under EN 1090-1 at execution class EXC2, with welding quality to EN ISO 3834-2. Units are hot dip galvanised to EN ISO 1461 and powder coated in RAL 7024.

Units are bolted, not welded, on site. A damaged panel is replaced as a single part without removing the frame or disturbing the planting.

The product is
the specification.

Case overview

A four year framework for street and park planters across a city centre and three district parks. Units are ordered in batches against a fixed catalogue and delivered to a nominated depot.

The award weighted whole life cost, corrosion protection and delivery reliability. Delivery reliability is certified on sheet 2.
