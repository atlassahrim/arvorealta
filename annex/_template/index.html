Case overview

The City of Helsinki Public Works Department's framework agreement covers street and park infrastructure: manhole covers, grates, bollard bases and kerb line units.

The tender evaluation weighted life-cycle cost, load class (D400/E600) and delivery reliability. This supplier was selected as the most economically advantageous.
