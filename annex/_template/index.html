01 · Scope of the work

The existing chain conveyor had been in service for nineteen years and could no longer hold line speed through the paint booth transfer. The replacement was specified, fabricated and installed without stopping production for more than one shift.

All welded assemblies were produced under EN 1090-1 factory production control, with welding to EN ISO 3834-2.
