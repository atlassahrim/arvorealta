## The evaluation scores the document

An evaluator sits with a scoring template and your submission, and awards points
for what is present, findable and specific. They cannot award points for what
you know and did not write down. They are not permitted to.

The paperwork is a separate object from the company.

A Norwegian road contractor argued the same thing from the other end. Mesta
complained to KOFA that the authority had assessed how each supplier scored on
the documentation requirements rather than on the award criterion itself, and
had given weight to matters no documentation was ever asked for.

That is two countries, two industries, and the same complaint. It is not an
accusation of unfairness. It is a description of the machine, and the machine is
why the feedback you get back names the outcome and never the line.

*KOFA 2023/472, Mesta AS mod Rogaland fylkeskommune*

## Before you say it is all price anyway

Sometimes it is. In Latvia between 2021 and 2023 the winner of 73% of
procurements was picked on lowest price alone. If that is your market, stop
reading. You are right and I have nothing to sell you.

Norway is the other end. Since 1 January 2024 contracting authorities there have
had to weight climate and environmental criteria at a minimum of 30%, and in the
first year around 75% of tenders carried it. A third of the score, decided on
documents, before anyone opens the commercial envelope.

Most of the tenders you bid sit between those two. The honest question is not
whether price matters. It is how much of the score was never about price, and
whether you have ever seen that part written down.

## What a document standard is

The set of things your firm asserts about itself, written once, properly, in the
form an evaluator scores. Capability, process, references with the detail that
makes them count, environmental and quality evidence stated as figures rather
than adjectives.

It is not a management system. Nothing changes in the workshop. It belongs to
you, your own people edit it, and the next tender starts from it instead of from
nothing.

## We manage without them

A Swedish firm said that to a trade body survey, with pride rather than regret.
We manage without them. We get paid in full by private clients, completely
without hassle.

They are not wrong. Public work argues about scope, pays on its own schedule,
and costs you a week of unpaid writing before anyone looks at your price. Of the
small firms staying out, 17% say it is too complicated and takes too long. Only
32% take part at all.

One number complicates it. The average number of bidders per procedure fell from
5.7 in 2011 to 3.2 in 2021. The field is thinning, and it is thinning for the
reason above. Firms are leaving because the paperwork became unreasonable, not
because they stopped being able to do the work.

So the contest is getting easier to win at the same time as it is getting harder
to enter. Everyone who withdraws hands their share to whoever can still produce
the documents.

You bid three times a year. That will not change and it does not need to. A firm
bidding constantly can absorb a bad submission, because there is another one
next month. Yours carries a third of your public work, and the one you lose on a
paperwork date is not replaced.

Bidding more is not the offer. The offer is that the few you do bid stop being
lost on the part nobody explained to you.

Before you spend €2,400 finding that out, spend nothing.

## Build note

**What changed from the published page, and why.**

- **Proof restored to the hero.** A market at stage three on the claim rejects a
  seventh assertion and cannot reject a dated ruling. The case now sits in the
  standfirst instead of one fold below it.
- **P. Olesen's own sentence is back in the body**, pulled up from a link label.
  It is the only supplier verbatim on the page.
- **Mesta's complaint is back, with its case number**, and is stated as a
  complaint rather than as a finding. `profile.md` marks it "still a candidate,
  not a finding."
- **The feedback fold is gone.** It was four sentences of assertion about the
  reader's own mind, with no evidence, and `voice.md` contradicts it: "No source
  in either pass contains a supplier admitting they do not know why they lose."
  Its one defensible point, that the buyer explains nothing, is now carried by
  P. Olesen's own testimony in fold 1 and one clause in fold 2.
- **"I have nothing to sell you" restored** to the Latvia fold. Without it the
  disqualification keeps the bluntness and loses the credibility it was buying.
- **"A questionnaire so that I can sell you the answers" restored**, with the
  free-consulting line under it. This is the page's answer to the binding risk.
- **The competitor anchor restored.** It is the only axis on the page with a
  named player at the far end, which is what rung 07 actually tests.
- **The do-nothing concession restored to a full paragraph.** Treating an
  objection as rational means showing the work, not stipulating it.
- **Eight links cut to three, and moved below the close.** A link mid-argument
  is an exit. A link after the close is a footnote for the reader who already
  agrees.
- **Source lines split where two sources were doing the work of one.** The
  published page attributed the bidders-per-procedure figure to Företagarna by
  proximity. Företagarna is the source for 32% and 17% and nothing else.

but trace to files that are not in this repo.**

- 15,000 kr for a single bid draft, cited to `competitor.md`
- 5.7 to 3.2 bidders per procedure, cited to `notes.md`

Cut the sentence rather than publish either one unchecked. The page's whole
strategy is that every number on it can be looked up.
