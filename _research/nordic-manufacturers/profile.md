## 2. The trigger

`[VERIFIED]` **Norway is the only hard, dated, in-force trigger in the
geography.** From **1 January 2024** Norwegian contracting authorities must weight
climate and environmental criteria at **minimum 30%**, with deviations requiring
justification in the procurement documents and described as rare. Adoption
reported at approximately **75%** of Norwegian tenders during 2024 (`trigger.md`).

`[VERIFIED]` **Regulation (EU) 2026/718, in force 20 March 2026**, requires four
environmental criteria — carbon footprint, durability, recyclability, efficiency
— as mandatory specifications for clean-technology public contracts. This is
**exclusion, not scoring**: without the accrediting documentation the bid is
removed. In force under six months (`trigger.md`).

`[VERIFIED]` **What cannot be claimed as a trigger, and each was checked at
source:**

- **The EU Public Procurement Act of 9 September 2026 is a proposal**, a single
  Regulation intended to replace three directives. It must pass Parliament and
  Council. **Nothing may describe it as law.** Its 30% and 50% quality minimums
  are proposed, not in force.
- **The Construction Products Regulation starts no clock for anyone yet.**
  Article 95(9) reaches a product family only one year after an implementing act
  makes a harmonised standard mandatory. **No family has started.** Recorded in
  `trigger.md` as an error that was caught and corrected — it had been wrong in
  the direction that flattered the offer.
- **Norway and Iceland are EEA, not EU.** EU instruments reach them only through
  EEA incorporation, on a separate and later timetable.

`[VERIFIED]` **A counter-trigger, and it belongs in the objections.** The CSRD
Omnibus value chain cap: from **3 July 2026**, a CSRD-obligated company may not
demand more sustainability data from a supplier of 1,000 or fewer employees than
the VSME defines — roughly **50 datapoints** in the Basic Module, with ESRS
datapoints cut by about **70%** and **85% fewer** companies in scope. The reader
may have read that the burden was cut and concluded the problem is over.

`[VERIFIED]` **Where the protection stops, which is the live tension:** the cap
covers CSRD-driven requests only. It does not touch public procurement award
criteria and it does not touch commercial conditions of doing business. *"A
shipowner can still require LCA data, product carbon footprints, or EPDs as a
condition of doing business."*

- **How long it has been live:** `[VERIFIED]` Norway's 30% rule, two years and
  eight months. Regulation 2026/718, under six months.
- **What happens if they do nothing for another cycle:** `[VERIFIED]` In Norway,
  they continue to forfeit up to a third of the available score before the
  technical evaluation begins. `[INFERRED]` Elsewhere, nothing visible happens,
  which is the problem.
- **External deadline, and whose:** `[VERIFIED]` **None that is honestly theirs.**
  Every dated instrument above binds the *contracting authority*, not the
  manufacturer. **Any deadline pressure in the copy would be manufactured.**

- **Awareness stage** — `[CLIENT]` `[VERIFIED]` **Problem aware on the burden,

- **Opening must** — enter on the reader's own position in their own register:

- **Opening must not** —
  - use any of the six dead claims, and **especially not claim 3**, *you are
    losing points you do not know you are losing*. It is true of this reader and
    it is the most-repeated sentence in the category.
  - claim any deadline. **Every dated instrument binds the authority, not the
    manufacturer.** Urgency here would be manufactured.
  - call the EU Public Procurement Act law. It is a proposal of 9 September 2026.
  - cite the Construction Products Regulation as a live obligation. No product
    family has started its Article 95(9) clock.
  - argue rising competition. Contradicted: 5.7 bidders per procedure in 2011,
    3.2 in 2021.
  - use the words *quality management system* or anything that scans as one.
    Företagarna's respondents name complicated quality management systems as an
    **unreasonable requirement**.
  - cite any England material, or treat the five countries as one regime. Norway
    is EEA, not EU.

- **Problem mechanism this reader has never had explained** — **CANDIDATE, NOT A
  FINDING. Must be confirmed true before it goes near a reader.**
  *The evaluation scores the document, not the company.* A bid can be cheapest
  and fastest and still lose on **when the quality-assurance paperwork was
  scheduled to arrive** — five days, in P. Olesen, on the record. A supplier can
