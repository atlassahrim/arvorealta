Price 20 · Quality 50 · Climate and environment 30. **80% non-price**, 9,000,000

https://app.artifik.no/procurements/6419/details

`[VERIFIED]` **Better on every criterion except the one that matters.** Artifik

at the wrong reader.

`[VERIFIED]` **The trade-off, stated plainly: the tender with the best data access
has the wrong bidders, and the tender with the right bidders needs a free
registration to read.** Registration is the cheaper problem.

## What stands in the way

`[VERIFIED]` **The documents need a free Mercell registration.** The public
permalink gives the description, volumes, value and dates. The scoring grid
detail, the four attachments and the Q&A need *"Show interest"*. Nothing was paid
and nothing is blocked, but it has not been done yet.

`[VERIFIED]` **The real deadline is 19 October, not 27 October.** Questions close
eight days before tenders do. A template that lands after the 19th cannot be
informed by what bidders asked, and `notes.md` names the published Q&A as the one
place the demand is already in writing.

`[VERIFIED]` **Latvia and Estonia produced nothing usable at this weighting.**
Their high-non-price tenders in the window are defence procurement run by Valsts
aizsardzības loģistikas un iepirkumu centrs and state agencies, not local buyers.

## One problem with the plan as written

`[INFERRED]` **Sending the same response template privately to ten bidders in one
live procurement is the wrong shape, and it is avoidable.**

Ten structurally identical submissions arriving at one evaluator invites the
conclusion that the bidders coordinated. In EU and EEA competition law a third
party that supplies the common instrument can be treated as a facilitator rather
than a bystander. The bidders carry that risk, not the sender, which makes it
worse rather than better as a way to open a relationship.

Three changes keep the strategy intact and remove the problem:

1. **Publish the template openly** on arvorealta.com instead of sending it to a
   selected ten. Open trade literature is not a secret arrangement, and the
   demonstration still happens. Then point the named suppliers at the page.
2. **Keep it a structure, not a script.** Section order, evidence tables, a layout
   calibrated to the 35/35/30 grid, and the question each table answers. **No
   pre-written answer text.** A format cannot make two submissions substantively
   identical; supplied prose can.
3. **Spread the private sends across the 78-award pool**, one bidder per live
   procurement, rather than ten inside this one.

`[INFERRED]` This rests on the structure of the plan rather than on any legal
advice, and it is worth twenty minutes with someone who does this for a living
before the first send.
