### 2. The trigger

- **What changed recently that made this live:** `[VERIFIED]` Six dated instruments in `trigger.md`, and **only one of them binds this reader today.**
  - **1 January 2024 — Norway, anskaffelsesforskriften § 7-9.** Climate and environment weighted minimum 30%, follow-or-explain, for procurements announced on Doffin from that date. ~75% adoption reported during 2024. **Live.**
  - 20 March 2026 — Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/718: clean-technology public contracts must accredit carbon footprint, durability, recyclability, efficiency. **Exclusion, not scoring.** Live for that category only.
  - 9 September 2026 — Public Procurement Act presented. A proposed single **Regulation** replacing Directives 2014/24, 2014/25 and 2014/23. Quality minimum 30%, 50% labour-intensive, comply-or-explain. **Passed neither Parliament nor Council.** Earliest application ~2029–2030.
  - 8 January 2026 — Construction Products Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 applies generally. **Obliges no manufacturer to declare GWP on that date.** Article 95(9) reaches a product family only one year after an implementing act makes a harmonised standard mandatory; three standardisation requests exist and no implementing act. **No family has started its clock.**
  - 19 July 2026 — ESPR full application, EU Central DPP Registry live. `trigger.md` states plainly: framework switch-on, **not yet a product obligation.**
  - 3 July 2026 — VSME adopted; value chain cap binding. **This one argues against the offer** — a manufacturer under 1,000 employees may now refuse most CSRD-driven sustainability questionnaires.
- **How long has it been live:** `[VERIFIED]` Norwegian manufacturer: two years and eight months. Clean-tech manufacturer in the 2026/718 categories: under six months. **Everyone else: announced, not binding.**
- **What happens if they do nothing for another cycle:** `[VERIFIED]` Norway and the 2026/718 categories: scoring loss or exclusion, now. `[INFERRED]` Everywhere else: nothing visible happens, which is the harder problem.
- **Is there an external deadline, and whose is it:** `[VERIFIED]` **18 February 2027 — the battery passport**, for EV, industrial (>2 kWh) and light-means-of-transport batteries only. The only fixed product-passport date in the pack. For any other category the deadline belongs to the buyer, not the law. `[INFERRED]` Buyer expectation moves ahead of legal obligation because delegated acts run 18–24 months ahead of compliance dates. **`trigger.md` flags this as plausible and unevidenced. Two research passes did not change that. Do not put it in front of a reader.**
