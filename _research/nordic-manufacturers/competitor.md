# Competitor — the seller side and the adjacent categories

Who sells into this market, how they position, what they charge, and which
adjacent categories compete for the same budget.

**Market:** industrial manufacturers · **Offer being researched:** document
standards for public tenders and industrial partnerships
**Geography:** Finland, Sweden, Norway, Estonia, Latvia, England
**Created:** 2026-09-16. This file did not exist before today. The first two
research passes wrote `voice.md` and `trigger.md` and never wrote this one, so
the seller side had never been recorded anywhere.

Sources are kept separate within this file. Each section is one source: what it
is, the URL, the date retrieved, the extracted material, then tagged
observations held apart from that material.

## A note on which seller side this is

`[VERIFIED]` Retrieved 2026-09-16. The first search run in Finnish —
*tarjouspyyntö konsultointi tarjousasiakirjat* — returned **North Patrol** and
**Dominus Hankinnat Oy**, both of which sell to the **contracting authority**:
they help a municipality write the *tarjouspyyntö*, specify requirements and
evaluate the bids that come back.
https://northpatrol.fi/palvelumme/julkisten-hankintojen-konsultointi/ ·
https://dominushankinnat.fi/tarjouspyynto/

`[VERIFIED]` That is the opposite side of the table from Arvorealta's reader.
The supplier-side market is reached with different words — *anbudsskrivning*,
*tilbudsskriving*, *bid writing*, *pakkumuse koostamine* — and returns an
entirely different and much larger set of firms. **The two markets share
vocabulary and share almost no vendors.**

`[INFERRED]` Anyone researching this market in the buyer's vocabulary will
conclude the category is small and lawyer-dominated. It is neither. Rests on the
contrast between the Finnish search above and the Swedish and Norwegian searches
below, run the same day with the supplier-side term.

## Competitor — Tender as a Service (Sweden)

**What it is:** A Swedish anbudsskrivning firm selling productised bid drafts at
a published fixed price. The only seller located anywhere in this research that
publishes a number on its own website rather than quoting on request.
**URL:** https://www.tenderasaservice.se/
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16. **Language:** Swedish.

### Source material (extracted)

Pricing, verbatim:

> "15 000 kr"

stated as the ordinary price per *anbudsutkast* (bid draft), excluding VAT, with
a *lanseringserbjudande 50%* (launch offer, 50%) running against it. Delivery:

> "Leverans av word-dokument i komprimerad fil inom 2 arbetsdagar"
> *Delivery of a Word document in a compressed file within 2 working days.*

For everything else: *"Priserna varierar beroende på omfattning och typ av
tjänst"* — prices vary by scope and type of service.

The problem, in their words:

> "Många företag har rätt erbjudanden men saknar tid eller resurser för att navigera i processen."
> *Many companies have the right offering but lack the time or resources to navigate the process.*

Claims carried on the page: *"600+ Skrivna anbud till olika branscher"* (600+
bids written across industries) and *"20+ års erfarenhet av upphandlingar och
affärsutveckling"* (20+ years of experience in procurement and business
development).

### Observations

- `[VERIFIED]` **15 000 kr excluding VAT for one bid draft, delivered in 2
  working days.** At the ECB reference rate this sits near the top of
  Arvorealta's €600 rung and well under the €2,400 rung. Same URL, 2026-09-16.
- `[VERIFIED]` What is sold is **one draft for one tender**. The deliverable
  expires when that tender closes. Nothing on the page offers a reusable asset.
- `[INFERRED]` The 50% launch discount against a published list price is a
  signal the list price is untested rather than a signal of generosity. Rests on
  the discount being open-ended and carrying no stated end date.
- `[INFERRED]` "Rätt erbjudanden men saknar tid" — *right offering, no time* — is
  the market's standard framing of the problem, and it is a **capacity** framing.
  It says the company is fine and merely busy. That is a different diagnosis from
  the one Arvorealta's offer rests on, and the two cannot both be the opening.

## Competitor — Anbudspartner (Sweden)

**What it is:** A Swedish anbudsskrivning firm positioning on craft and on
writing against the scoring matrix rather than on capacity.
**URL:** https://anbudspartner.se/anbudsskrivning
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16. **Language:** Swedish.

### Source material (extracted)

The positioning line, verbatim:

> "Anbudsskrivning är ett hantverk med tydliga regler: svara exakt på det som frågas, bevisa varje påstående och skriv mot poängmallen."
> *Writing bids is a craft with clear rules: answer exactly what is asked, prove every claim, and write against the scoring template.*

The argument for why it matters:

> "En medioker text kan kosta mer poäng än ett högre pris."
> *A mediocre text can cost more points than a higher price.*

Terms: *fast pris innan start* (fixed price before start), no figure published.
*Färdigt på 5 till 7 arbetsdagar* (finished in 5 to 7 working days).
*Kostnadsfri första bedömning* (free first assessment). *Revideringar ingår*
(revisions included), described as *"Ni granskar och godkänner, vi reviderar
tills ni är nöjda"* — you review and approve, we revise until you are satisfied.
And on ownership: *"Texterna är era. Allt vi skriver får ni återanvända fritt"* —
the texts are yours, everything we write you may reuse freely.

### Observations

- `[VERIFIED]` **This is the closest thing to a stated mechanism found on the
  seller side in any of the six countries.** *Answer exactly what is asked,
  prove every claim, write against the scoring template* is a causal account of
  why bids score badly, not a claim about the vendor. Same URL, 2026-09-16.
- `[VERIFIED]` *"En medioker text kan kosta mer poäng än ett högre pris"* is the
  single sharpest competitor line located. It is the argument Arvorealta would
  otherwise expect to make first, and a Swedish competitor is already making it
  on a public page.
- `[VERIFIED]` **"Texterna är era... får ni återanvända fritt" is reuse offered
  as a term of business, not as a product.** The client may reuse the text; the
  vendor does not build the thing that makes reuse work. Same URL.
- `[INFERRED]` *Unlimited revisions until satisfied* prices the vendor's time
  risk into an unbounded commitment. Arvorealta's €2,400 includes **one**
  revision round. Against this competitor that reads as restrictive unless the
  difference in what is being bought is made explicit early. Rests on comparing
  the two terms directly; no Swedish buyer was heard reacting to either.

  between those two readings is where an opening exists. Rests on the claim
  inventory in `notes.md`.

anskaffelser Norge*
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16. **Language:** Norwegian.

### Source material (extracted)

| Firm | URL | Own framing |
|---|---|---|
| Din Anbudshjelp AS | https://dinanbudshjelp.no/ | Founded 2014. *"har Din Anbudshjelp AS levert et høyt antall komplette anbudsbesvarelser på vegne av våre kunder, og kontrakter for mange millioner er vunnet"* — *has delivered a high number of complete bid responses on behalf of our clients, and contracts worth many millions have been won* |

| Anbudsjuristen | https://www.anbudsjuristen.no/ | Started 2017 by lawyers Lene Harling Myrold and Heidi Langseth. From *granskning av konkurransegrunnlaget* (review of the tender basis) through writing and quality assurance before submission |

### Observations

- `[VERIFIED]` **Anbudsjuristen was founded by two named lawyers**; VerdiNor sells
  to both sides of the table. The Norwegian field leans legal and procedural
  where the Swedish field leans commercial. Same search, 2026-09-16.
- `[VERIFIED]` *"kontrakter for mange millioner er vunnet"* — **contracts worth
  many millions won** — is an unquantified proof claim. No figure, no client, no
  date. https://dinanbudshjelp.no/
- `[INFERRED]` A firm selling documentation discipline that itself publishes a
  rounded, unattributed proof claim is a live demonstration of the gap
  Arvorealta's offer describes. Whether a Norwegian manufacturer reads it that
  way is unknown and untested.
- `[VERIFIED]` **Anbudsjuristen publishes an *anbudsmal* — a bid template.**
  https://www.anbudsjuristen.no/aktuelt/anbudsmal/ — This is the nearest thing in
  the Norwegian field to a reusable asset, and it is given away as marketing for
  the per-bid service rather than sold. **Not retrieved in detail; worth a
  dedicated fetch**, because a free competitor template is the most direct
  substitute for a paid document standard.

## Competitor — the England field and its published rate card

**What it is:** The English bid-writing market, which unlike the Nordic one
publishes detailed fee guides as content marketing. The richest pricing source in
this file.
**URL:** https://tsaksconsulting.com/blog/how-much-does-a-professional-bid-writing-service-cost-in-the-uk-2026-and-2027-fee-guide/
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16. **Language:** English. Firm: Tsaks Consulting.

### Source material (extracted)

Published fee bands:

| Unit | Fee |
|---|---|
| Bid review / compliance QA | £400–£950 |
| Small or simple tender | £500–£2,500 |
| Standard SME public tender | £2,500–£5,000 |
| Large framework or major contract | £5,000+ |
| Day rate, junior or independent freelancer | £300–£500 |
| Day rate, senior sector specialist | £500–£750 |
| Tsaks' own rates | £450 junior, £700 senior |

The line that matters most, verbatim:

> "Engaging a professional bid and tender writing fees depend on your existing policies and documentation"

Positioning: *"we do bring the added advantage of having multiple bid writers,
senior bid writers, and also graphic designers and other coordinators on our
team."*

### Corroborating range from a second English source

**URL:** https://www.tenderconsultants.co.uk/bid-writing-cost/ and
https://assuredbid.co.uk/in-house-vs-outsourced-bid-writing-the-real-cost-of-building-a-bid-function-in-2026/ ·
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16, via search result summary rather than direct fetch.

Day rates £400 to £600 mid-level, £600 to £850 senior, £700 to £1,000+ for
specialist sector consultants; freelance £550 to £1,200. Fixed per-bid fees for
an SME-scale public tender *"most often land somewhere between £1,500 and
£7,000"*. Enterprise and multi-lot programmes £35,000 to £150,000+.

### Observations

- `[VERIFIED]` **"Fees depend on your existing policies and documentation."** A
  competitor states, as its own pricing logic, that the absence of a document
  standard is what makes each bid expensive. Same URL, 2026-09-16.
- `[INFERRED]` That sentence is the strongest third-party support located for
  Arvorealta's entire premise, and it is **in a competitor's rate card rather
  than in a buyer's mouth**. It proves the vendor believes it. It does not prove
  the manufacturer has noticed. Those are different rungs and only the second one
  sells.
- `[VERIFIED]` **The English market prices per bid, from £400 to £150,000+, and
  charges by the day.** A day rate is the clearest possible statement that the
  thing sold is labour, recurring, and re-bought every cycle. Same sources.
- `[VERIFIED]` **Contradiction to record, not to resolve.** Tsaks puts a standard
  SME public tender at £2,500–£5,000. The AssuredBID and tenderconsultants
  material puts the same unit at £1,500–£7,000. Overlapping but not identical
  bands, from firms with an interest in where the band sits. Do not state a
  single English per-bid price.
- `[INFERRED]` Arvorealta's €2,400 lands inside the English standard-SME-tender
  band for **one** bid. Priced against England, the full document standard costs
  what a single English tender response costs, and does not expire. That
  arithmetic is available and has not been used anywhere on the offer page.

## Adjacent category — proposal content-library software

**What it is:** The category that actually sells the reusable asset. It exists,
it is large, and it is priced for a different company than Arvorealta's reader.
**URLs:** https://loopio.com/pricing/ ·
https://www.answerpath.com/resources/blog/rfp-response-software-pricing-in-2026-loopio-vs-responsive-vs-answerpath
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16, via search result summary. **Language:** English.

### Source material (extracted)

Loopio: a central library of answers, AI auto-fill for recurring questions.
Estimated at **$54k–$142k annually**, quoted rather than published, no free
trial. Responsive: tiered SaaS **$19 to $65 per user per month**, 14-day free
trial, aimed at large multi-region teams handling high volumes of RFPs and
security questionnaires.

On maintenance, across platforms:

> "keeping the library accurate is a manual job with content owners reviewing answers to catch conflicts, outdated content, and duplicates"

### Observations

- `[VERIFIED]` **The reusable-asset category exists and is not empty.** Anyone
  claiming nobody sells reuse is wrong. Same sources, 2026-09-16.
- `[VERIFIED]` **It is priced and scoped for high-volume RFP teams**, per seat,
  per year, forever. $54k–$142k a year is not a number a mid-size Nordic
  manufacturer bidding a handful of tenders a year will price against a €2,400
  document.
- `[INFERRED]` The gap is real and specific: **software sells the container and
  charges rent; the bid writers sell the contents one tender at a time and charge
  per bid; nobody located in six countries sells the contents once, as an owned
  asset.** That is the positioning axis. Rests on the whole of this file.
- `[VERIFIED]` The maintenance line above names the failure mode of the software
  answer: a library nobody curates fills with conflicts and stale content. A
  document standard that is set once and not maintained has the same failure, and
  the offer should not pretend otherwise.
- `[VERIFIED]` **Pricing tagged as estimate, not as published.** The $54k–$142k
  figure is described in the source as *"based on quotes rather than published
  pricing"*. Do not state it as Loopio's price.

## Adjacent category — EPD and environmental documentation

**What it is:** The competing documentation spend a manufacturer actually faces,
driven by the green award criteria recorded in `trigger.md`. This is the budget
line Arvorealta is nearest to and furthest below.
**URLs:** https://epd.guide/epds-and-the-bottom-line/epd-cost-in-2026-expect-about-17k-to-55k ·
https://forestsource.io/resources/guide/publication-cost-epd-international ·
https://circularecology.com/how-much-does-it-cost-to-produce-an-epd.html
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16, via search result summary. **Language:** English.

### Source material (extracted)

Programme operators: EPD International, IBU (Germany), **EPD Norge
(Scandinavia)**, EPD Australasia, EPD North America.

Full cost of a verified EPD, 2026: *"around USD $17,000 to $54,000 per
product."* A second figure, from a Shobatake et al. 2024 study restated at 2026
values, puts combined consultancy and verification at **$13,401–$37,251**.

Registration and publication fees under the International EPD System are a
separate and much smaller line.

### Observations

- `[VERIFIED]` **$17,000 to $54,000 per product, per EPD.** A manufacturer facing
  green award criteria on a product family spends multiples of Arvorealta's
  €8,000 identity system on environmental documentation alone. Same URL.
- `[VERIFIED]` **Contradiction to record, not to resolve.** On registration fees
  the sources conflict outright: one gives *"EUR 1,000 for the first EPD... then
  a volume discount down to EUR 50 by the hundredth"*; another gives *"one EPD
  costs €69, two EPDs cost €138"*. These cannot both describe the same fee.
  **Neither figure may be stated to a client** until read off the programme
  operator's own published price list.
- `[INFERRED]` This cuts both ways and the direction is not settled. Against the
  EPD line, €8,000 reads as small and easy to approve. But the EPD is compelled
  by regulation and the document standard is not, and a budget holder funds the
  compulsory one first. **Which effect dominates is unknown and is a question for
  a real buyer.** Rests on the price comparison only.
- `[VERIFIED]` **EPD Norge is named as the Scandinavian programme operator** and
  was not researched directly. Its published fee schedule is the correct primary
  source for any Norwegian or Swedish number and has not been read.

## Adjacent category — prequalification registers

**What it is:** The standing supplier registers that already ask a Nordic
manufacturer to assemble and maintain a documentation set, annually, for a fee.
The nearest existing thing to a recurring document obligation.
**URLs:** https://achilles.pages.salesfusion.com/Achilles-JQS-Supplier-Order-Form-En (**HTTP 400 on fetch, 2026-09-16**) ·
https://infinity-ex.com/achilles-jqs-registration-atex-supplier-guide-norway/
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-16. **Language:** English.

### Source material (extracted)

Achilles **JQS** (Joint Qualification System) is described as a supplier register
and pre-qualification system used by buyers in the Norwegian and Danish oil and
gas sector. **Sellihca** is described as an industry-based scheme established to
improve efficiency and reduce costs through the supply chain for both buyers and
suppliers, and to ensure supplier applications are handled fairly and
consistently by subscribing utilities.

On fees:

> "The qualification fee is to be paid in advance (VAT to be added for suppliers situated in Norway), and is an annual qualification fee based on the selected number of codes. The fee covers one year's qualification and is non refundable."

### Observations

- `[VERIFIED]` **The fee is annual, scales with the number of codes selected, is
  paid in advance and is non-refundable.** The structure is recorded; **no
  amount was obtained.** The order form returned HTTP 400 on 2026-09-16.
- `[VERIFIED]` **This is a genuine gap in this file.** Achilles fee levels are
  the most relevant recurring-cost anchor in the Nordic half of the geography and
  they are not in hand. Named as the first thing to fetch on any further pass.
- `[INFERRED]` A manufacturer already paying an annual Achilles fee has already
  accepted the premise that standing documentation costs money every year. That
  reader needs less convincing on the category and more on why a one-time asset
  beats another subscription. Rests on the fee structure above; no supplier was
  heard describing an Achilles renewal.
