# Partner studios: the first list

**Built on 8 November 2026** from Clutch's country pages, web searches and
each studio's own homepage, read for this file.

**The rule:** keep a studio that builds websites, products or marketing but
does not sell brand identity itself. A studio that already sells identity is
a competitor, not a partner. Each homepage was searched for brand, identity
and logo words in English, Norwegian, Finnish and Estonian, and every hit was
read in context.

**Fit:**

- **A:** their clients plausibly have an €8,000 budget (startups, scale-ups,
  B2B).
- **B:** they serve small local businesses on €290 to €2,000 sites, where
  €8,000 is a big ask. Contact these last.

**Contact:** what the studio's own site shows, as of 8 November. A named
address is better than a generic one. Where none is shown, use the contact
form or find the founder on LinkedIn.

**Not yet done, and the owner's to do:**

- one real project per studio and the first line about it;
- the founder's name, where the site does not give it.
