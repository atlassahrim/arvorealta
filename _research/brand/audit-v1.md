# /brand/ — audit v1

English copy, six folds, 1 November 2026. No profile file for this market, so
rungs 09 to 06 are judged from the page's own evidence and from the design read
in CLAUDE.md. Rungs 05 to 00 are judged in full.

**Caveat the skill asks for:** folds one, two and four were edited in the same
session that is auditing them. The English is the owner's; the structure and the
Nordic are this session's. A fresh conversation would be a stronger read.

## 1 — The break

**Rung 08. The solution mechanism is shown and never stated.**

The system fold is the page's only candidate for a causal explanation, and both
of its prose lines are negations:

- headline: `Tools are the easy part.`
- explainer: `Direction is often mistaken for micromanagement. We need clean
  vectors, not 30 page ruling documents.`

Beneath them sit four rules — `The synthesis`, `Ten, twenty, seventy`,
`One typeface`, `The culture stack` — with no sentence saying what they are or
why they produce a mark that survives. The fold shows a specification and never
calls it one.

CLAUDE.md already records the cause: the line that did this work
(*Four rules, on one project, all the way down*) was replaced and nothing took
over its job.

**Why it is a break and not a preference.** The reader is at sophistication
stage five: every studio claims longevity, so claims are spent and only
mechanism, demonstration or identity moves anyone. The page has demonstration
(the two plates) and identity (the sorting line). It has a **problem** mechanism
— *most projects are made for the short term and perceived wind direction* —
which is non-obvious and removes blame. The solution half is a list of four
deliverables with no causal claim attached. On this market that is the one rung
that cannot be skipped.

Three negations in a row across folds three and four — *not tools*, *not
micromanagement*, *not a 30 page rulebook*, *Not here* — is the symptom. A page
defines itself by what it is not when the positive claim has gone missing.

## 2 — The fix

One sentence, above the grid, stating what the four rules do. It costs nothing
structurally: the fold, the grid and the headline all stay. The owner's
micromanagement objection stays too, demoted below the claim it was standing in
for.

## 3 — The rewrite

The system fold's explainer. Eyebrow and headline unchanged.

> **The system**
> **Tools are the easy part.**
>
> A mark drawn for an audience is redrawn when the audience moves. These four
> hold it still. That is not micromanagement and it is not a 30 page rulebook.
> It is four decisions, made once.

Four sentences against two, inside the same fold height. Every word of the
owner's objection survives; it now answers a claim instead of standing in for
one. `made once` is the hinge into the close, which already reads *it happens
once*.

## 4 — Notes

Preferences, not breaks. None of these is why anyone leaves.

- **`History` over the gallery** promises a chronology the fold does not have.
  It is one picture at a time with a name, a year and a sector — a room, not a
  timeline. `The work` is honest and `Seventeen marks` is better.
- **`Win with class.`** is the one abstract noun on a page that earns everything
  else concretely, and the one line that would sit unchanged on any studio's
  site. The fold under it is specific; the headline is not.
- **The years and sectors are the page's level-two proof** — checkable
  specifics, fifteen of them — and they sit in the quietest type on the page.
  That is the right restraint for a portfolio and worth knowing it is a choice.
- **Rung 00 is clean.** `Winds change.` is the only short sentence in the hero
  standfirst and it lands because of it. One action, named with a verb, used
  everywhere: `Start a project`.
