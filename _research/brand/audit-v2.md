# /brand/ — audit v2

1 November 2026, live copy, English first and the Nordic checked for parity.
There is still no `profile.md` for this market, so rungs 09 to 06 are judged
from the page and CLAUDE.md, not checked against a profile. This session wrote
none of the page. It read `audit-v1.md` only after reaching its own verdict.
The two audits agree on the rung, and this one sharpens the evidence.

## 1 — The break

**Rung 08: the page explains why brands fail and never says why this one
lasts.**

- **09 passes.** The reader is a decision maker sent by a referral, aware of
  the solution, and at sophistication five: every studio says "timeless".
  `You set direction.` opens on identity, which is the one thing a stage-five
  market still answers to. The sorting line (`If the difference is not
  obvious to you…`) is the same move, and it works.
- **The big idea passes the test.** Leave the studio out and it still changes
  what the reader believes: *the audience is the wind, not the destination.*
- **The problem mechanism passes.** `Most designers abide by whichever
  audience is easy to see.` It is not obvious, and it takes the blame off the
  reader.
- **The solution mechanism is missing.** The system fold reads:
  > `Tools are the easy part.`
  > `Direction is often mistaken for micromanagement. We need clean vectors,
  > not 30 page ruling documents.`

  Under that sit four rule **titles** and nothing else. `p1b` to `p4b`, the
  bodies, are in `STR` but no node renders them. No line on the page connects
  the four rules to a mark that lasts. The causal sentence had already been
  written, in `p4b`: *A mark survives because the things around it agree with
  it.* It is dead.

The fold spends its only sentence answering an objection
(*micromanagement*) before stating the claim that would raise it. That is
also a rung-03 inversion, but it follows from the 08 break. At stage five
the reader cannot skip the mechanism. Without it, the plates and the gallery
read as taste, not as method, and taste is what every competitor claims too.

## 2 — The fix

State the mechanism in the space the fold already gives one sentence. Lift
the dead `p4b` claim, call the four rules the means, and keep the owner's
objection as the answer to that claim. Make it no longer than it must be,
because mandatory snap traps a reader in any fold taller than the screen.

## 3 — The rewrite (applied)

> **The system**
> **Tools are the easy part.**
>
> A mark lasts when everything around it agrees with it. These four rules
> are that agreement. Not micromanagement. Not a 30 page ruling document.

- nb: *Et merke varer når alt rundt det er enig med det. Disse fire reglene
  er den enigheten. Ikke detaljstyring. Ikke 30 siders regelverk.*
- fi: *Merkki kestää, kun kaikki sen ympärillä on sen kanssa samaa mieltä.
  Nämä neljä sääntöä ovat se yhteisymmärrys. Ei mikromanageerausta. Ei
  30-sivuista sääntökirjaa.*

The sentence lengths run long, mid, short, short. The two clipped negations
keep the owner's bluntness instead of sanding it down. The fold measures 1.00
screens at 1440, 1180, 820 and 390 in all three languages.

## 4 — Notes

Preferences rather than breaks:

- **The hero's "we" collides with "you".** `You set direction` is followed
  by `We do not negotiate direction`. CLAUDE.md defines "we" as the client
  and the partners, but a cold reader hears the studio refusing the client's
  direction, which is the opposite of the headline. `Nobody negotiates your
  direction.` fixes it in four words.
- **`History` is the eyebrow over the gallery.** It promises a chronology the
  fold does not have. `The work` is plain; `Seventeen marks` is better, but
  only fifteen are in the room, so `Fifteen marks`.
- **`The high end standard.` and `Win with class.`** are the two lines that
  would fit any studio's site unchanged. Everything else on the page earns
  its point concretely.
- **System comes before Direction** in the DOM and in the rail. That puts the
  solution ahead of the problem it solves. It is an owner decision, and the
  fix above survives either order.
- **The Norwegian close runs 1.04 screens at 1440 and 1.03 at 1180**, before
  and after this change. Mandatory snap makes that a small trap on the last
  fold. It is a layout issue, not copy.
- **Rung 00 is clean.** There is one CTA verb throughout, `Start a project`,
  and `Two lines is enough. I read these myself.` is the best piece of
  effort-risk handling on the site.
