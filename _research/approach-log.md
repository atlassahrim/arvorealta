## The pattern across all three, worth keeping

**Krav 57 defeated every one of them, in the same word.**

| Company | What their document says |
|---|---|
| EnviroPac / SULO | «inntil 100 prosent PCR» |
| Namdal / Craemer | «bis zu 90 Prozent hochwertigem Rezyklat» |
| Strømbergs / PWS | «opptil 100 % resirkulert plast» |

Three unrelated manufacturers in three languages, and the krav asks a
*forpliktende minimumsandel* per part with a third-party certificate. **Every
one of them offered a ceiling where a floor was asked for**, on the
requirement carrying 30 of the 100 points.

That is not a coincidence and it is the single most valuable thing this
project has learned. **It is the product.** A supplier cannot see it because
each of them only reads their own document; it is visible only from where
three have been read side by side.
