# Namdal Ressurs — what the catalogues show

Read from screenshots the owner captured on 8 and 9 October 2026: four pages
of the Craemer *Hjulbeholdere* catalogue (Norwegian, Issuu only, **not
downloadable**), an index page of an SSI Schäfer catalogue (English,
downloadable, **102 pages**), and **pages 54–63 of that catalogue in full** —
every four-wheel MGB page it has.

**This is screenshot evidence, not the documents.** Everything below is
visible in the captures and nothing is inferred from them.

## The structural fact that changes the pitch

Namdal Ressurs is a **distributor**, not a manufacturer. It sells at least
three brands of wheeled container — Craemer, SSI Schäfer and Helesi — and
**publishes no document of its own**. Every page the owner found carries
somebody else's logo.

EnviroPac's problem was that their datasheet answered 9 of 34 requirements.
Namdal's is one level up: **there is no document that says what Namdal Ressurs
offers Valdres.** To bid they must assemble one from two foreign catalogues,
one of which cannot be downloaded. That is a better fit for the paid tiers
than EnviroPac was, and it should shape the review and the email.

## Craemer, four pages

- **Sizes, MGBneo 120–360 l:** 120, 140, 180, 240, then 340/360 on a separate
  row. **No 80 l in that family** — it sits in a separate MGB 60–80 l range.
- **Sizes, MGBneo⁴ 660–1100 l:** 660, 770, 1100, and 1100 Lid-in-Lid.
- **The standard marked is `DIN EN 840`**, as a logo beside each family.
  Krav 3 and 4 ask for **NS-EN 840 del 1-6 with documentation**. No part
  numbers, no certificate number, no issuing body, no date.
- **No EN 1501-5 anywhere**, which is krav 5 and an A-krav.
- **Material, verbatim:** *«… å produsere holdbare og gjenbrukbare produkter
  med kun ren, høykvalitets PE … Craemer plastpaller, pallekasser, plastbokser
  og hjulbeholdere er derfor alle laget av høykvalitets PE.»*
- The sustainability passage beside it is about **taking products back at end
  of life**, not about recycled content going in.
- **Colours are named, not numbered:** anthracite, blue, green, yellow, brown,
  red as swatches. **No RAL code appears**, where krav 41 asks for RAL 7016.
- Marking options: logo, letters and sequential numbering, all by heat stamp.
- Quality assurance: internal laboratory, external institutes named only in
  the abstract.

### What Schäfer does better than EnviroPac, and it has to be said

The review has to be fair or it is not checkable, and on two requirements this
catalogue is **stronger** than the SULO sheet DR-001 scored.

- **Krav 3 and 4 — NS-EN 840 del 1-6.** Schäfer names the parts in prose,
  which SULO never does: *«comply with the European Standard DIN EN 840-2 /
  840-5 / 840-6»* (p60), *«conform to the European Standard EN 840-3 / 840-5 /
  840-6»* (p62), *«made in compliance with DIN EN 840-2»* (p59), *«in
  accordance with DIN EN 840-6»* (p62). The `Euro Norm EN 840 – 1-6` mark
  appears on pages 55, 58, 60 and 63. **Still partial, not answered** — krav 3
  and 4 both read *«Leverandør bes bekrefte, samt vedlegge dokumentasjon»*,
  and there is no certificate number, no issuing body and no date anywhere.
  But it is a document that knows the standard has parts.
- **Krav 18 — wheels.** *«solid rubber tyres, individual swivel wheels of
  200 mm diametre»* (p61), and `Wheel-Ø mm 200` in every one of the four
  tables. The krav asks a minimum of 200 mm on four-wheel containers, so this
  is **answered**. It also says larger wheels are viewed positively, and 200
  is the floor exactly.
- Material is named: **HDPE**, *«compact injection moulding with High Density
  Polyethylene (HDPE)»* (p61), with *«special UV stabilisation»* (p60, p62).
  Krav 8 asks for *gjennomfarget* UV-stabilised polyethylene — through-coloured
  is not stated, so partial.
- Both plastic families carry the **RAL AWB Gütezeichen** for waste and
  recycling containers (p60, p63). That is not krav 6, which asks for
  **RAL-GZ 951/1** by name, but it is the same family of mark and closer than
  anything in the SULO sheet.

### The finding that leads, and it is now verified

Krav 14 is an **A-krav**:

> «Beholdere på 4 hjul skal være 660L og 1000L.» — *Leverandør bes bekrefte.*

**No 1000 litre four-wheel container appears anywhere in pages 54–63.** The
sizes are 660, 770 and 1,100, in every family and every table. Craemer runs
660 / 770 / 1100. The Schäfer index adds a 500. **Neither brand Namdal
distributes shows the size the requirement names.**

**The buyer writes an equivalence when they mean one, and they did not write
one here.** Krav 13 says 370 l is wanted and 360 l accepted, in those words.
Krav 41 says RAL 7016 *«eller tilsvarende»*. Krav 8 says *«eller
tilsvarende»*. Krav 14 carries nothing — just *confirm it*.

It is also the size EnviroPac publish a current datasheet for, which is how
DR-001's closing footnote could name all six: 80, 140, 240, 360, 660 **and
1000 litres**.

**What the review may say** is that the published catalogues do not show it.
**What it may not say** is that Namdal cannot supply one — they are a
distributor, these are two of at least three brands, and a catalogue is not a
price list. The claim is about the documents, which is the only claim this
project ever makes.

### The second finding, and it was already verified

Krav 57 is an A-krav under the criterion weighted **30 of 100**:

> «Plasten i beholderkroppen skal inneholde PCR. Forpliktende minimumsandel
> PCR (vekt-%) oppgis separat for beholderkropp, lokk og hjul … dokumenteres
> med tredjeparts sertifikat … Fabrikkspill regnes ikke som PCR.»

- **Schäfer: absent.** Ten pages on the four-wheel range and no recycled
  content claim of any kind. HDPE, UV stabilisation, corrosion resistance,
  child safety, wheel options — no PCR, no percentage, no certificate.
- **Craemer: worse than absent.** *«kun ren, høykvalitets PE»* — pure,
  high-quality PE. That is a document asserting the opposite of what the
  heaviest criterion requires.

### The third, and it is about the documents themselves

Krav 2 is an A-krav: *«Det vedlegges produktblad / brosyrer over beholderne.»*

- **Craemer is an Issuu flipbook.** It cannot be attached to a Mercell
  submission at all.
- **Schäfer is 102 pages** of which four are the relevant tables. It is
  attachable, and it is not a product sheet.

### Colour, krav 41

RAL 7016 **or equivalent**, dark grey, body without lid.

- Schäfer p60 names colours as `grau · blau · gelb · grün · braun · rot`, with
  the footnote *«Colours are similar to those illustrated. Some colour
  deviation is possible.»*
- Schäfer p63 names them `black-grey · pale brown · green · signal white ·
  sky blue · rape yellow`.
- **No RAL number appears on any of the ten pages.** Two different colour
  vocabularies appear within three pages of each other.

Because krav 41 carries *«eller tilsvarende»*, this is partial rather than
absent — but a bid has to state the equivalent, and the catalogue gives the
bidder nothing to state.

## What is still needed before DR-002 is built

1. **The Schäfer catalogue as a file.** The owner says it downloads. The
   screenshots prove the finding; they cannot build the sheet. DR-001's plates
   are cut at `pdftoppm -r 200` from the source PDF, and a photograph of a
   screen is not that. **Every page of it also has to be read**, not ten —
   the two-wheel sizes krav 11, 12, 13, 19 and 25 ask about are in the GMT
   range elsewhere in the 102 pages, and scoring 34 requirements off the
   four-wheel section is the DR-001 error wearing a different coat.
2. **Whether a Craemer PDF exists anywhere.** Still unanswered, and it decides
   whether krav 2 is a finding or the finding.
3. **Which brand Namdal would bid.** The review has to score one document. If
   it cannot be known, the review says so on the page.
