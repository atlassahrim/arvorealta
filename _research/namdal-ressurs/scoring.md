compactors, lifts and Røros Produkter's indoor furniture. None is a wheeled

## Why a sales catalogue is the right thing to score

The owner asked, and it belongs on the record.

1. **Most findings here are product facts, not document facts.** A 1,000 litre
   bin built to DIN 30760 and a 200 mm wheel stay true whatever document is
   written next. They came out of the suppliers' own spec tables.
2. **Krav 2 asks for exactly this.** *«Det vedlegges produktblad / brosyrer
   over beholderne.»* Brochures. The tender asks for the published material.
3. **Where it is an evidence gap, absence in marketing is strong evidence of
   absence in the file.** Marketing publishes its best facts. The exception
   proves it: Craemer's German brochure carries the recyclate figure and the
   Norwegian one does not, which is finding 01.

**What the review must not do** is score this as if it were their bid
response. It is not. Twenty-eight of the thirty-four read *Leverandør bes
bekrefte* or *Beskriv løsning*, and those confirmations are written fresh.
The sheet therefore asks what an evaluator can **verify** from the published
set, not what Namdal will claim.

## The four findings

**01 · Krav 57, A · climate 30 of 100 — two documents, one manufacturer.**

The Norwegian catalogue Namdal publishes, page 3:

> «… å produsere holdbare og gjenbrukbare produkter med **kun ren,
> høykvalitets PE** …»

Craemer's own German brochure, `BranchenfolderMGB_2024_DE_final_web.pdf`,
created 10 April 2024:

> «Craemer Produkte werden aus **bis zu 90 Prozent hochwertigem Rezyklat** in
> verschiedenen Farben gefertigt.»
> «Auf Wunsch sind Craemer MGB mit dem Umweltzeichen **Blauer Engel**
> lieferbar.»

Same containers. The document that reaches a Norwegian evaluator is the one
that says pure PE, and pure PE is the answer that loses krav 57.

**This is a documentation gap, not a product defect, and that is what makes
it the lead.** It costs nothing to fix and it is worth a share of the
heaviest criterion. Craemer is listed in the Blauer Engel product database as
*Müllgroßbehälter MGB 60-1100L, HDPE, anthrazit*, under *Products made from
Recycled Plastics*.

**Two things the review must not say**, and both were nearly said:

- **Not that the four-wheel bins lack the Blue Angel.** On the Norwegian
  catalogue's page 1 the mark sits on MGBneo 120–360 and not on MGBneo⁴
  660–1100 — but the Blauer Engel listing covers *MGB 60-1100L*, so the
  absence is in the catalogue's logo column, not established in the product.
  The sheet says what the page shows and stops.
- **Not that 90 % answers krav 57.** *Bis zu* is *up to*, and the krav
  explicitly wants a *forpliktende minimumsandel* per part with a third-party
  certificate. It is a far better starting point, not an answer.

**02 · Krav 14, A — the 1,000 litre is a different lifting system.**

Krav 14: *«Beholdere på 4 hjul skal være 660L og 1000L.»* No equivalence
clause, where krav 13 writes one for 370/360 and krav 41 writes *eller
tilsvarende*.

Craemer's four-wheel range is 660 / 770 / 1100. Schäfer's EN 840 ranges are
660 / 770 / 1100. The one 1,000 litre in 106 pages is the **DU MGB** on
Schäfer p41, whose table footnote reads *«all dimensions are nominal in
accordance with DIN 30760»* — the only table in the catalogue that does not
cite EN 840.

And p40, introducing that range:

> «The waste disposal system of the future … **Instead of a comb lift** the
> refuse vehicles are fitted with a diamond lift (or a combination of both
> systems).»

Krav 5, A: the buyer's trucks have **kamløft**.

**Say the catalogues, not the company.** Namdal is a distributor of at least
three brands and a catalogue is not a price list. The claim is that the
published material does not show a 1,000 litre comb-lift container.

**03 · Krav 18, A — the small bins' wheels.**

Krav 18: *«minimum diameter 250 mm (tohjuls) og 200 mm (firehjuls)»*.

Schäfer p29, the GMT table: `Wheel-ø mm 200 · 200 · 200 · 200 · 200 ·
200/300` across 60, 80, 120, 140, 240, 360 l. Three of the four two-wheel
sizes the buyer must have are specified at 200 mm. Only the 360 offers 300.

Four-wheel is met everywhere at 200 mm, which the sheet says, because a
review that only finds faults is not checkable.

**04 · Krav 2, A — what can actually be attached.**

Craemer: four pages, December 2024, an overview with no dimensions, no
weights and no wheel figures. Schäfer: 102 pages, published to Issuu
**9 August 2019**, a general catalogue across balers, depot containers and
skips in which the relevant tables are four pages.

The date is the checkable part and it is stated as a date, not as a claim
about whether Issuu will let a file be saved — the owner reported being able
to download one of the two, and the reader API does not settle it either way.

## What is credited, and it matters

- Schäfer names EN 840 **by part**, which the SULO sheet DR-001 scored never
  does
- Four-wheel wheels are 200 mm in every table — krav 18 met
- HDPE and UV stabilisation named
- RAL quality-association membership with a named annual auditor (SKZ
  Würzburg), on both brands
- GMT bins stated as comb-lift compatible and EU 2000/14 conformant
