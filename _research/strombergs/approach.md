## The one line that is different, and why

`The widest gap is the one worth 30 of the 100 points.`

The first two emails carried the count alone. This one carries the count and
**points at where the money is**, because the count here is good news — twelve
of thirty-four would read as a compliment and nothing else, and a compliment
is not a reason to open a file.

**It does not say what the gap is.** That stays in the PDF, which is the rule
the first two emails already follow: the email contextualises the attachment
and never summarises it.

**It does not say twelve is the highest of three.** The sheet does not say it
either. Saying it would tell him his competitors are getting the same
approach, which contradicts the sentence above it.
