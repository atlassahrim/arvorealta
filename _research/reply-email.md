# The reply email — what to send the hour somebody answers

Four approaches are out and nothing was prepared for a reply. This is that.

**It is one template with one variable**, which is the count. EnviroPac 9,
Namdal 5, Strømbergs 12, all of 34.

## What the reply has to do, in order

1. **Hand over the thing that expires**, which is the question to the buyer.
   Written questions close **19 October**. Anything else can wait a week; this
   cannot, and it is the only part of the reply with a clock on it.
2. **Correct the 30 % to 10 %.** Unprompted. See below.
3. **Name the price and what fits before the 27th.**
4. **One next step.**

**It does not re-sell the review.** They have read it. A reply that re-explains
the attachment is a reply that has nothing new.

## The three decisions inside it

**The correction goes in unprompted, and early.** Being precise about a figure
that was loose in your own favour is the single most persuasive thing a
document-standards firm can do in a first reply, and it costs nothing — 10
and 10 is still 20, and 20 named exactly beats 30 named loosely. **If they
find it themselves first, the whole offer is worth less.**

**The question is given away with no strings, and the fact that the answer is
public is stated.** It would be easy to let them think it buys them an edge.
It does not, the competition document says so, and a firm whose product is
documents that survive scrutiny cannot open a relationship with a claim that
does not.

**€600 is the offer and €2,400 is named but deferred.** Thirteen days is
enough for one corrected document and is not enough for a document standard.
Offering the €2,400 for this deadline would be selling something that cannot
be delivered. The credit mechanic does the rest of the work — per CLAUDE.md,
€600 comes off €2,400 in full.

## What is deliberately not in it

- **No new findings.** They have the two pages. A reply that adds a fifth
  finding says the review was incomplete.
- **No apology for the correction.** One sentence, the right number, move on.
- **No meeting request.** `Say when and I will call` puts the next move with
  them and costs them one word to accept.
- **No mention of the other two companies.** The one-company promise is live
  against three, and the first to say yes takes the slot. If a second says
  yes afterwards, they get a straight answer that it is taken — which is
  what the promise meant.
