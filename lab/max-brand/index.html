Turvatikas

Safety ladder

Turvatikas make safety ladders. The mark is four forms stacked and what you read is three rungs: the ladder drawn as the space between them rather than as the rails.

The lockup, as it is drawn

The mark

Four lens forms, stacked. The three gaps between them are the rungs. Nothing in it is thinner than the thinnest thing beside it, which is what lets it go small without a second drawing.

The wordmark

TURVATIKAS, SAFETY, LADDER. Three lines of tracked capitals in a serif, ranged left, set to the height of the mark beside them. The name is not abbreviated and the trade is not hidden.

The lockup

Mark left, wordmark right, on one optical centre. It is the arrangement every surface here uses, which is what makes the three grounds below read as one thing rather than three.

One drawing, three grounds. The colour moves to meet the ground. The construction does not move at all.

The card

Amber through chocolate on a forty five degree axis, the lockup reversed out in the palest step of its own gradient. The price gradient on this firm's own homepage is sampled off this card.
