Brands for decision makers

You set the brand, not your audience.

Most marks fade away within five years, made for the short term and perceived wind direction. Winds change. We do not negotiate direction. We pick the winds to ride.

Can you see the difference?

A mark is a statement read before it is explained. The test is clarity, not meaning. It is impact in the second before you think.

If the difference is not obvious to you, nothing written here will make it so.
