09 questions

Questions you should ask me.

Can I check the numbers on this page?

Most of them, now. The Danish ruling is published under J.nr. 25/03606 and the Norwegian one under KOFA 2023/472, both searchable by case number. The Latvian and Norwegian weighting figures come from published sources. Two are weaker and I would rather say so than let you find out. The 15,000 kr comparison and the fall from 5.7 to 3.2 bidders per procedure are drawn from my own research notes and I have not yet re-verified either against the original source. Treat them as indicative until I have.

Is this a quality management system?

No, and it is the wrong thing to compare it to. A management system changes how you work and then asks you to keep proving that you do. This is a document. Nothing in the workshop changes, nobody is audited, and there is no annual renewal. If a supplier has told you that complicated quality management systems are an unreasonable requirement, they were right, and this is not one.

What happens if my losses really are about price?

Then you have wasted a page and I have wasted an afternoon, which is the whole reason the first step is free. Send a page from a tender you lost. If it comes back scored and the score was fair, you have your answer and it did not cost you anything to get it.

Who actually does the work?

I do. There is no team behind this page and no account manager. That is a real constraint and it is why the engagement is four hours of your time rather than a project, and why there is a limit to how many of these run at once.

Do you have clients I can speak to?

Not yet in this segment, and I am not going to invent one. That is a fair reason to start with the free scored page rather than with €2,400, and it is the honest reason the ladder starts where it does.

Do you work outside the Nordics?

The work is built around Finnish, Swedish, Norwegian and Danish procurement practice, and that is where I can be useful today. Elsewhere in the EU is a direction rather than a readiness claim I will make now.
