I was wrong about this earlier. I said a shadow on ink has nothing to fall onto. The real reason B's shadow vanished there is that I tinted it with ink, rgba(23,53,44,.24), and that colour composited over the ink ground returns #17352C exactly, a ratio of 1.00. It was invisible by construction, not by physics. Ink is not black, so a black shadow does register, at 1.31 against the ground where the same shadow reaches 1.56 on paper. Weaker on ink, and not impossible.
