A commercial proposal in Brazilian Portuguese for a van conversion, written as two A4 sheets rather than an email. Four levels of scope, each crediting in full into the one above, so a client who climbs the whole ladder pays the price of the top rung and not the sum of four.

02 two systems, kept apart

Editorial

Cinzel, Playfair Display and Shippori Mincho on warm greige, ink green and taupe. Three grounds, read in order: paper argues, taupe carries the evidence, ink makes the statement. It is for presenting a concept.

Technical

IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono on white stock. Facts are set as fields — a mono label with the value under it — so an evaluator scanning for a sum or a date finds it without reading. It is for documents that get scored.

The two share exactly one value, the accent red. That is what keeps them recognisably the same hand while letting each do a job the other cannot.
